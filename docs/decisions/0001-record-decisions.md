# 0001 — Record decisions

**Date:** 2026-09-15
**Status:** accepted

## Context

Conclave is built largely with AI assistance. The risk is a working system whose design I cannot explain — which fails both as engineering and as a portfolio project, where the questions are "why does this activity heartbeat?" and "what happens if a worker dies mid-merge?"

## Decision

Every non-obvious choice gets a record here, written by me in my own words after the ticket merges, not generated. Numbered, short, stating context, decision, and consequences — including the ones I do not like.

## Consequences

A small ongoing cost per ticket. In exchange, the reasoning survives, and writing it is the check on whether I actually understood what I merged.
